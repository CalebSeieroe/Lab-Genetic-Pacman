Ghosts Behavior

Modes

  • Scatter: Ghosts move to their corners of the maze. No ghost will chase Pac-Man.
  • Scared: When Pac-Man eats a power pellet, the ghosts turn blue. The ghosts movement is random and slower.
  • Chase: Ghosts will chase Pac-Man. Each ghost has a different strategy to chase Pac-Man.
